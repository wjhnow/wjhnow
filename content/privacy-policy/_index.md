This Privacy Policy explains how What Just Happened NOW ("we," "us," "wjhnow.com") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our site.

## Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to read our content. However, some information is collected automatically:

- **Analytics data:** We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This may include your approximate location, device type, browser, pages visited, and time spent on the site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this data.
- **Advertising data:** We may display ads served by Google AdSense and/or other ad networks. These networks may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data at [Google's Privacy & Terms page](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Newsletter (if applicable):** If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address solely to send you updates. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email we send.

## Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them (directly or through third-party services like Google) to:
- Understand site traffic and usage patterns
- Serve relevant advertising
- Remember basic preferences

You can disable cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may affect how some parts of the site function.

## Third-Party Services

## Your Choices

- You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- You can opt out of personalized advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) or via [YourAdChoices](https://youradchoices.com).
- EU/UK visitors have rights under GDPR to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold (such as a newsletter email).

## Children's Privacy
