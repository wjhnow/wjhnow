## Who Writes This

Our writers use editorial pen names (Mr. JH for science, Mr. Wnow for tech) and illustrated avatars. The names are pen names, the avatars are illustrations, and the work behind each article is real. You can read more on our [About page](/about-wjh.now/).

## How We Research

- We start from primary sources: research papers, official agency releases, and original data.
- Every article ends with direct links to the sources it relies on, so you can check us.
- We prefer the original study or announcement over a news write-up of it.
- When something is uncertain, early-stage, or debated, we say so instead of presenting it as settled.

## How We Use AI

We use AI tools to help with research and drafting. Every article is reviewed, fact-checked against its sources, and edited by a human before it is published. AI tools do not publish anything on their own.

## What We Don't Do

- We don't invent quotes, experts, or credentials.
- We don't publish sponsored content disguised as editorial.
- We don't chase breaking news. We would rather be right than first.

## Corrections

We get things wrong sometimes. If you spot an error, email [info@wjhnow.com](mailto:info@wjhnow.com) with the article link and what needs fixing.

- Factual errors are corrected as soon as we verify them.
- Significant corrections are noted at the bottom of the article with the date.
- Typos and small wording fixes are made quietly.

## Independence

Our editorial decisions are our own. If we ever add advertising or sponsorships, they will be clearly labeled and will not influence what we write.
