**Last updated:** October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WJH.now (short for What Just Happened NOW, "we," "us," "wjhnow.com") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our site.

## Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or give us personal information to read our content. Some information is collected automatically:

**Analytics data:** We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This may include your approximate location, device type, browser, pages visited, and time spent on the site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this data. Google may process this data on servers outside your country.

## Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them (directly or through Google) to:

- Understand site traffic and usage patterns
- Remember basic preferences

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Doing so may affect how some parts of the site work.

## Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics, which has its own privacy policy: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We do not currently show ads. If that changes, we will update this policy before ads go live.

## Your Choices

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

If you are in the EU or UK, you have rights under GDPR to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold. Email us and we will respond.

## Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
